## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, quiet-luxury digital boutique for modern Indian women. The experience channels the sensory tactility of bespoke leathercraft, polished metallic hardware, and heritage artisanal vanity cases into a refined mobile-first surface.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Poised & Graceful:** Restrained, deliberate pacing that resists aggressive conversion tropes. It lets silhouette, grain, and structural proportion speak.
- **Warm Sensuality:** Replaces cold, clinical minimalism with warm ivory washes, rose-gold undertones, and brushed champagne brass.
- **Connoisseurship & Trust:** Communicates heritage mastery through editorial typography, deliberate whitespace, and meticulous micro-details (e.g., dedicated artisan provenance labels and crisp INR currency lockups).

### Design Movement: Warm Modernist Minimalism with Tactile Depth
The visual style fuses strict editorial layout discipline with tactile luxury accents:
- **Spacious Negative Space:** Expansive padding that mimics high-end editorial lookbooks, giving vanity bags and organizers gallery-grade focus.
- **Subtle Metallic Cadence:** Champagne gold and rose-tinted trims are applied sparingly to active states, dividers, and key structural badges.
- **Restrained Surface Transitions:** Fluid sheet transitions and gentle layered elevations evoking soft leather paneling and velvet linings.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural unbleached silk, soft blush leather finishes, and jeweler's gold accents. The foundation uses high luminous warmth rather than stark electronic white, ensuring a tactile, bespoke feel throughout.

### Primary Roles
- **Primary Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm ivory foundation. All root screens, backdrops, and root scrollers sit on this tone to prevent eye fatigue and simulate premium archival paper.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FDFBF7`):** Soft luminous off-white used for cards, floating sheets, and product presentation pedestals.
- **Primary Ink (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep charcoal-black for core titles, product names, prices, and primary interactive buttons. Delivers sharp contrast while remaining softer than pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary Ink (`#5C554E`):** Warm graphite for secondary descriptors, specifications, and bag dimensions.
- **Subtle Borders (`#EFEBE4`):** Whispering muted bone for clean hairline separators and structural card perimeters.

### Accents & Metallics
- **Rose Gold Accent (`#D48B93`):** Delicate blush-rose used for editorial highlights, seasonal badges, heart/wishlist fills, and refined notifications.
- **Soft Blush Base (`#F9EEF0`):** Ultra-light tint for chip backgrounds, discount pill bases, and promotional banners.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`):** Burnished metallic detail used for luxury badges ('Artisan Handcrafted', 'Limited Edition'), verified guarantee micro-icons, and gold metal hardware representations.

## Typography

The typography pairs the high-contrast editorial drama of **Playfair Display** with the pristine geometric clarity and open apertures of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Editorial Headlines (Playfair Display):** Reserved for collection names, high-impact storytelling, category banners, and hero statements. Maintain medium or regular weight; avoid heavy bold weights to preserve delicate serifs.
- **Interface & Mechanics (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs product titles, structural descriptions, functional inputs, and basket totals. Maintains high legibility even at compact mobile sizes.
- **Micro-Caps & Badges (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror gold-stamped interior leather tags (e.g., `ARTISAN HANDCRAFTED`, `LIMITED EDITION`, `GENUINE LEATHER`).
- **Currency Presentation (`₹`):** Always style the Indian Rupee symbol in crisp sans-serif matching the numerical price weight to avoid serif symbol clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around quiet luxury shopping behaviors, prioritizing one-handed ergonomics, sweeping product showcases, and breathing room.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins and gutters. Cards typically span full width for hero lookbooks or 2 columns (`grid-cols-2`) for dual product browsing.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `32px` margins and `24px` gutters. Product showcases expand to 3 balanced columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`max-w-7xl` centered) with `64px` margins and `32px` gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Maintain liberal vertical separation (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) between merchandising modules (e.g., between "Vanity Trunk Show" and "Handcrafted Totes").
- Keep micro-gaps within interactive card elements compact (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) so pricing, swatches, and labels remain tightly coupled to the imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface stacking complemented by ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient glows that mimic natural boutique lighting. Harsh drop shadows are strictly avoided.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Neutral warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`). Flat backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & List Surfaces):** `#FDFBF7` bounded by a 1px ghost border in `#EFEBE4`. Zero spread, shadow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(92, 85, 78, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts, Filter Drawers, & Dropdowns):** Lifted floating panels with shadow: `0px 8px 30px rgba(92, 85, 78, 0.07)`, reinforced with subtle rose-tinted top edge highlight.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar & Modal Sheets):** Mobile checkout triggers and bag details elevated using `0px -4px 32px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06)`, grounding actions above the fold.
- **Frosted Translucency:** Sticky top navigation uses `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` paired with a `16px` backdrop-blur and a bottom hairline divider in `#EFEBE4` for seamless scrolling over product imagery.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects the soft architecture of structured vanity luggage and tailored leather pouches: clean, structured, yet softened at every transition point.

### Radius System
- **Core Elements (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Applied to standard interactive cards, text input fields, and bottom sheet containers.
- **Contained Touchpoints (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem`):** Applied to thumbnails, selector swatches, and inline alert banners.
- **Signature Editorial Elements (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem`):** Applied to featured lookbook story panels, image galleries, and vanity case comparison cards.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for category filter pills, metadata tags (`Bestseller`, `Artisan Handcrafted`), counter badges, and the primary "Add to Vanity Bag" sticky buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Bag", "Proceed to Checkout"):** Solid deep charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) background with warm ivory text (`#FAF8F5`). Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), min-height 52px on mobile. Active state compresses scale to `0.98` with subtle rose glow.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Explore Story", "Personalize"):** Outlined 1px solid `#1A1A1A` with transparent background, tracking `0.05em`.
- **Icon Actions (e.g., Wishlist Heart, Share):** 44x44px circular base with `#FDFBF7` surface, bordered with hairline `#EFEBE4`. Heart fill activates with Rose Gold (`#D48B93`).

### Badges & Tags
- **Artisan / Heritage Badges:** Pill shape, soft ivory surface, 1px border in Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`), typography set to `label-caps` in `#C5A059`.
- **Merchandising Tags ("Bestseller", "New"):** Subtle Blush background (`#F9EEF0`) with Rose Gold text (`#D48B93`), weight 700.

### Product & Vanity Cards
- **Image Presentation:** 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratio with softened corners (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`). Warm neutral photo backdrops with subtle zoom transition on tap/hover.
- **Card Metadata:** Left-aligned. Category in `label-caps` (`#5C554E`), Product Title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, Price clearly defined in `currency-price` with `₹` symbol followed by subtle color-swatch discs.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Soft off-white base (`#FDFBF7`), subtle border (`#EFEBE4`), height 50px with `0.75rem` radius. Floating or elevated labels in Plus Jakarta Sans. Focus state shifts border color to warm charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) with no default blue browser glow.
- **Swatches:** 28px circular leather/color swatches with a 2px offset white ring and an outer active halo in `#C5A059`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal circular rings with smooth rose-gold fill transitions and custom gold check marks.

### Luxury E-Commerce Specifics
- **Sticky Mobile Buy Bar:** Anchored to bottom viewport with dynamic blur, displaying instant INR price alongside a full-width pill button.
- **Compartment & Dimension Drawer:** Expandable visual tabs demonstrating vanity internal organizers, slot counts, and water-resistant lining details.
